## Loop pile or cut pile for offices?

**Loop pile** carpet has yarn looped back into the backing, so the surface stays firm under heavy foot traffic and chair castors. It is the usual choice for open-plan offices, corridors and meeting rooms.

**Tweed texture** mixes two yarn colours, which hides dust and small marks. It suits busy offices and hotel corridors.

**Soft cut pile** suits executive offices and quiet reception rooms, where traffic is lighter and comfort matters more.

## How to estimate office carpet

Carpet cost before installation = area in m² × price per m².

**Example:** an open-plan office of 100 m² (1,076 sq ft) needs about 100 m² of carpet plus a small allowance for cutting around columns and doorways, confirmed after measuring. Lightweight carpet costs from 125 KWD (1.250 KWD per m²) to 300 KWD (3 KWD per m²), and carpet tiles 500 to 600 KWD.
