## Steps to order

1. Send a photo or file of the pattern on WhatsApp, with the size and type of place.
2. We go over the pattern with you.
3. We confirm the price and size before starting.
4. We carve the pattern by hand and hand over the work.

## What we need from you

- A photo or file of the pattern.
- The size of the carpet or rug in meters (or feet).
- The type of place it is for.
- A photo of your existing carpet, if you want one carved.

## Carved and printed carpet

In carved carpet, the pattern is worked by hand into the carpet itself. In printed carpet, the pattern is printed onto the surface. Choosing one changes how the result looks, so send us the pattern you want and we explain which suits it.
