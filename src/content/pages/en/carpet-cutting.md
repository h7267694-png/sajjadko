## Steps from message to handover

1. Send the length, width and type of place on WhatsApp.
2. We agree the type and price by thickness, pile pressure and density.
3. We check the measurements with you, and arrange a site visit and measuring when needed.
4. We cut the carpet or wall-to-wall carpet to the confirmed size.
5. We install it if you ask for installation, and follow up for two days afterwards to fix any installation error.

## Where cutting to size is used

**A regular room.** We cut carpet or wall-to-wall carpet to the length and width of the room, so the room does not have to fit the roll.

**An irregular room.** If it has columns, corners or openings, we cut the edges around its shape after checking the measurements with you.

**A mosque.** We measure the hall on site and align the rows with the qibla, the direction Muslims face in prayer. Row width is 1.33 m (4.4 ft). If a wall is not square to the qibla, we cut the edges at an angle that keeps the lines straight.

**A prayer room in a company or school.** We tailor the carpet to each prayer room, because these rooms are often small and may be irregular.

## How cutting is priced

The quantity is measured in m², which is length times width. The price is the price of the type you choose: 5 to 15 KWD per m² (0.465 to 1.394 KWD per sq ft) for Turkish carpet and wall-to-wall carpet, set by thickness, pile pressure and density, and 6 to 8 KWD per m² for mosque and prayer room carpet. The installation fee is separate from the price per m². For the cost of cutting a special size, send the size on WhatsApp.

## What we need from you

- The length and width in meters (or feet, and we convert).
- The type of place: home, mosque or prayer room.
- Any columns, openings or irregular corners.
- A photo of the space if possible.
- Whether you want installation together with the cutting.
