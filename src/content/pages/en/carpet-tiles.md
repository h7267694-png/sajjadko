## Why carpet tiles for offices?

**Partial replacement.** If a spot wears out under a chair or by the door, you replace that tile instead of the whole floor.

**Access under the floor.** In offices with cabling or service hatches under the floor, tiles lift out and go back in.

**Flexible layouts.** If the office layout changes later, the tiles can be rearranged or extended to the new space.

## Nylon or polypropylene?

**Nylon** has resilient fibres that spring back after pressure, so it handles heavy foot traffic and chair castors and lasts longer. It suits open-plan offices, corridors and meeting rooms.

**Polypropylene** costs less and resists moisture and stains. It suits lighter-traffic rooms and large areas on a tighter budget.

## How to estimate carpet tiles

Number of tiles = area in m² × 6.25.

**Example:** a 50 m² (538 sq ft) office needs about 313 tiles, plus a small cutting allowance at walls and columns that we confirm after measuring. The tiles cost from 250 KWD (5 KWD per m²) to 300 KWD (6 KWD per m²), before installation.

## Carpet tiles or broadloom?

If budget matters most, lightweight broadloom carpet starts at 1.250 KWD per m² and gives a seamless surface; see [office carpet](/en/office-carpet/). If partial replacement and easy maintenance matter more, carpet tiles are the better choice.
