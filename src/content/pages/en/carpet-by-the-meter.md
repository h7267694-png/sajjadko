## How carpet by the meter is priced

Carpet cost = area in m² × price per m², then add the installation fee if you want installation.

The price is per square meter, which is length times width, not per linear meter. We start from the area of your space, then agree the right type and its price within the range of 5 to 15 KWD per m².

## What sets the price between 5 and 15 KWD

The price changes from one type to another by three factors.

**Thickness.** The height of the carpet or wall-to-wall carpet.

**Pile pressure.** How tightly the pile is packed.

**Density.** How dense the yarn is in the carpet or wall-to-wall carpet.

These factors come together in each type, so we agree the price once we know the place and how it is used. Send us the size and the type of place on WhatsApp and we explain the options available.

## How to measure your space

1. Measure the length and width of each room in meters. If you only have feet, send those.
2. Multiply length by width to get the area in m².
3. Add the areas together if the order covers more than one room.
4. Mention columns, openings and irregular corners, because they change the cutting.
5. Send the numbers on WhatsApp and we confirm the quantity.

## Cutting to size

We cut carpet and wall-to-wall carpet to the size of your room, so the room does not have to fit the roll. A cut cannot be undone, so we check the measurements with you before cutting. If the room is an irregular shape, we cut the edges to follow it.

## Installation

The installation fee is separate from the price per meter and is agreed by area and location. For two days after installation, we follow up to fix any installation error.

## Mosque carpet by the meter

Mosque carpet is priced separately from home carpet and wall-to-wall carpet. It costs 6 KWD per m² for light thickness, 7 KWD for medium and 8 KWD for thick. You will find the details on the mosque carpet page of this site.

## Steps to order

1. Send the length, width and type of place on WhatsApp.
2. We agree the type and price by thickness, pile pressure and density.
3. We cut the carpet or wall-to-wall carpet to size after checking the measurements with you.
4. We install it if you ask for installation.
