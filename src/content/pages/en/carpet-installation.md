## Steps from message to handover

1. Send the approximate area, district and type of place on WhatsApp.
2. We agree the type and price by thickness, pile pressure and density.
3. We arrange a site visit and measuring when needed, and coordinate with mosque management if the place is a mosque.
4. We cut the carpet or wall-to-wall carpet to the confirmed size and install it.
5. We follow up for two days after installation to fix any installation error.

## Where installation is used

**A home.** We install carpet or wall-to-wall carpet in your rooms after checking the measurements with you.

**A mosque.** We coordinate with mosque management to set the date for the visit and the installation. We fix the carpet with Botex, double-sided tape, or an iron carpet edge strip, depending on the floor. Whoever orders the carpet does not need to follow the installation personally, and the invoice is issued in their name after the work.

**A prayer room in a company or school.** Installation is available outside working hours, so work or classes are not interrupted. We tailor the carpet to each prayer room before installing it.

## How installation is priced

The installation fee is separate from the price of the carpet or wall-to-wall carpet, and it is agreed by area and location. The type itself is priced per m²: 5 to 15 KWD per m² (0.465 to 1.394 KWD per sq ft) for Turkish carpet and wall-to-wall carpet, set by thickness, pile pressure and density, and 6 to 8 KWD per m² for mosque and prayer room carpet. We do not give a fixed figure for the fee because it changes with area and location. Send the area and district on WhatsApp and we confirm the fee.

## What we need from you

- The approximate area in square meters (or square feet, and we convert).
- The district, and the type of place: home, mosque or prayer room.
- The type of floor if you know it.
- A photo of the space if possible.
