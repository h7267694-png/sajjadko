## Prayer Room Carpet by Building Type

A prayer room inside an existing building is often small. It can also be irregular in shape. So we tailor the carpet to each room, and we do not ask the room to fit the roll. Facilities or HR teams usually place the request, and we arrange the site visit and installation with them.

**Companies.** The room sits among offices and serves staff through the working day. We cut the carpet to the room and choose thickness and colour with you.

**Schools.** The school administration runs the room. We choose thickness by how heavily the room is used, and tailor the carpet to the space.

**Complexes.** A complex may have more than one prayer room. We tailor a separate carpet to each room.

**Hospitals.** The room sits inside a building busy with patients and visitors. So installation with minimal disruption matters.

**Men's and women's rooms.** If one building has a men's room and a women's room, we tailor the carpet of each to its own size. You can pick the colour of each room from more than 9 colours.

Thickness is your choice: light, medium or thick, within 12–30 mm (0.5–1.2 in). Full mosque halls are covered on the separate mosque carpet page.

## Finding the Qibla in a Room Without a Mihrab

The qibla is the direction Muslims face in prayer. A room in an office or school has no mihrab, the niche that marks it. So building management sets the qibla direction before we tailor the carpet. We do not offer a separate qibla-finding service. Once the direction is set, we align the rows with it during cutting and installation. Each row is 1.33 m (4.4 ft) wide. If the room walls are not square to the qibla, we allow for that when measuring and cutting.

## Installation Without Disrupting Work

We plan installation to disturb the building as little as possible. The stages are:

1. Site visit and measuring when needed.
2. Cutting the carpet to the room size.
3. Choosing the fixing: Butex, double-sided tape, or metal carpet end strips.
4. Installation. The installation fee is agreed by area and location.
5. Two days of follow-up after installation to fix any installation error.

Installation is available outside working hours.

## Mobile Musalla Carpet

A mobile musalla here means carpet tailored for a temporary or mobile prayer space. The number of rows and the length follow your request and the space available. There is no fixed number and no fixed length. Each row is 1.33 m (4.4 ft) wide, and the carpet is cut from a roll 1.33 m (4.4 ft) or 4 m (13.1 ft) wide. The service covers the carpet only. If you need a chalet or a complete mobile structure, that is negotiated separately based on the area and size. For the carpet, we tailor it to what you ask for.

## Prayer Room Carpet Cost

Carpet cost before installation = area in m² × price per m² for the thickness. The price is the same for the 1.33 m (4.4 ft) roll and the 4 m (13.1 ft) roll:

- Light: 6 KWD per m² (0.557 KWD per sq ft)
- Medium: 7 KWD per m² (0.650 KWD per sq ft)
- Thick: 8 KWD per m² (0.743 KWD per sq ft)

These prices are for mosque and prayer room carpet only. Turkish carpet and wall-to-wall carpet for other uses cost 5 to 15 KWD per m² (0.465 to 1.394 KWD per sq ft), by thickness, pile pressure and density.

Example: a room of 4 × 5 m (13.1 × 16.4 ft) is 20 m². The carpet then costs 120 KWD for light, 140 KWD for medium or 160 KWD for thick. The installation fee is separate and agreed by area and location.
