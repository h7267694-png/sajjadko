## Rug or Wall-to-Wall Carpet?

**A rug** defines the play corner and keeps the marble visible around it. It is easy to move and clean. Choose a size that leaves at least 40 cm of floor to the walls.

**Wall-to-wall carpet** is safer for small children: it is fixed, so it cannot slide or lift at the edges, and it softens falls and footsteps. A calm colour for the room with a bright rug on top works well.

## How to Choose a Kids Rug

- **Pile:** short and dense, soft to sit on and easy to vacuum.
- **Colour:** grey and beige hide marks; blue, green and pink brighten the room.
- **Size:** 120 × 180 cm for a play corner, 160 × 230 cm under a single bed, 200 cm round for a play circle.
- **Shape:** round and custom shapes are cut from carpet by the metre if no ready-made size fits.
